\item Suppose that salaries \( x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n \) (measured in units of the minimum wage) are independent and follow a simple Pareto distribution with probability density function
\[
f(x \mid \alpha) = \frac{\alpha}{x^{\alpha+1}}, \qquad x \ge 1,
\]
where \( \alpha > 1 \) is an unknown shape parameter.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item {[4]} Find the method of moments estimator $\hat{\alpha}_{\text{MM}}$.

    \item {[5]}  Let $ p \in (0,1] $ be the proportion of the population with the highest salaries. 
    Show that the share of total income received by this top \( p \) fraction of the population is 
    \[
    D(p) = p^{\frac{\alpha-1}{\alpha}}.
    \]

    \item {[1]} The empirical Pareto principle (the «80/20 rule») states that approximately 20\% of the population receives 80\% of the total income. 
    Using the formula find $\hat{\alpha}_{\text{rule}}$ that satisfies
    \[
    D(0.2) = 0.8.
    \]
    
\end{enumerate}


\item  Let \( x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n \) be a random sample from a binomial distribution \( \dBin(N, p) \) with probability mass function
    \[
    P(x_i = x) = C_N^x p^x (1-p)^{N-x}, \quad x = 0, 1, 2, \ldots, N.
    \]    
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item {[4]} Find the method of moments estimators for $N$ and $p$.
    \end{enumerate}
    Consider known $N = 4$ and the observed sample \( (4, 1, 2, 2, 1) \).
    \begin{enumerate}[resume]
        \item {[3]} Find the MLE estimate of $p$.
        \item {[2]} Prove that MLE estimator of $p$ is unbiased and consistent.
        \item {[1]} Find a minimal sufficient statistics for $p$.
    \end{enumerate}

\item  The depth of corrosion $\mu$ [mm] on a metal plate is measured using four different unbiased independent methods (ultrasonic, electrical resistance, optical, and X-ray). 
    Due to the different principles and equipment, the measurement variances are known to be
    \[
    \sigma_1^2 = 1.6,\quad \sigma_2^2 = 2,\quad \sigma_3^2 = 2.5,\quad \sigma_4^2 = 3.
    \]
    The measured values [mm] of obtained by the four methods are
    \[
    x_1 = 19,\quad x_2 = 18,\quad x_3 = 20,\quad x_4 = 21.
    \]    
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item {[3]} Give at least one example of an unbiased estimator \( \tilde{\mu} \) of \(\mu\).

        \item {[4]} Find the linear unbiased estimator \( \hat{\mu} \) of \( \mu \) that has the smallest possible variance.

        \item {[1]} Compute the numerical value of \( \hat{\mu} \) using the given data.

        \item {[2]} Compute the standard error of \( \hat{\mu} \).

    \end{enumerate}

\item Random variables $X_1$, \dots, $X_n$ are independend with Poisson distribution $\P(X_i = k) = \exp(-\lambda)\lambda^k/ k!$ for non-negative integers $k$.
Random variables $Y_1$, \dots, $Y_n$ are independend with $\P(Y_i = 1) = p$ and $\P(Y_i = 0) = 1-p$.

I do not observe $X_i$ nor $Y_i$, I observe only their product $W_i = X_i Y_i$. 
Among $n=1000$ observations of $W_i$: $300$ observations are exactly equal to $0$ and $\sum W_i = 7000$.

Test the null hypothesis $p=1$ against alternative $p<1$ using likelihood ratio test with significance level $0.05$. 

FYI: right $0.05$ critical chi-squared values are $\chi^2_{1,\text{crit}} = 3.84$, $\chi^2_{1,\text{crit}} = 5.99$, $\chi^2_{1,\text{crit}} = 7.81$.
